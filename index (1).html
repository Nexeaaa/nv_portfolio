From the analyst

I sit between the people who need a system and the people who build it.

Neca Vae P. GalarpeBS Information Technology, STI College Cagayan de Oro

I study Information Technology at STI College Cagayan de Oro and made the Dean’s List from 2023 to 2025. In my capstone group I am the systems analyst and technical writer. I interview stakeholders, map how work really gets done, and turn it into functional specifications a development team can build from.

Beyond the classroom I have led student organizations of over 300 members, served on the board of Club 404, and worked in a barangay records office, where accuracy and confidentiality are the job. I joined city-wide youth consultations and received a Certificate of Service Award from the City Government of Cagayan de Oro.
